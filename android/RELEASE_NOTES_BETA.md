### 🇮🇩 Ringkasan Rilis Beta / Pre-Release
* **Kanal Pembaruan Pengujian (Beta Channel)**:
  * Memungkinkan beta tester menguji fitur-fitur eksperimental, perbaikan algoritma pembacaan NFC, dan peningkatan antarmuka sebelum rilis stabil publik.
  * Tetap mempertahankan penyimpanan riwayat kartu lokal (*in-place upgrade* tanpa menghapus data kartu yang tersimpan).
* **Fitur & Perbaikan Dalam Pengujian**:
  * Peningkatan kestabilan pembacaan chip multi-kartu uang elektronik.
  * Dukungan deteksi update dual-channel langsung dari aplikasi.

### 🇬🇧 Beta / Pre-Release Notes
* **Beta Testing Channel**:
  * Enables beta testers to preview experimental features, NFC parsing engine refinements, and UI optimizations prior to general public release.
  * Preserves local stored card history and settings seamlessly (*in-place upgrade* without data loss).
* **Features & Fixes in Testing**:
  * Enhanced stability for multi-card electronic money chip reading.
  * Direct dual-channel update check support from within the application.
